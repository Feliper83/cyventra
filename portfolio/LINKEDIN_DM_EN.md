# LinkedIn Prospecting DM Sequence — English Version

> **How to use**: this is a 3-step sequence — connection note, first message, follow-up. Personalize every `[bracket]`. Send these yourself, one prospect at a time — do not automate sending on LinkedIn (violates their Terms of Service and risks your account). See a legitimate way to *find* prospects (Sales Navigator, Apollo.io) before using this.

---

## Step 1 — Connection request note (300 character limit)

Hi [Name] — noticed [Company] handles [specific repetitive task] manually. I help small businesses fix exactly that with a fixed-price AI automation sprint. Would love to connect.

*(Count your characters before sending — LinkedIn cuts off connection notes at 300.)*

---

## Step 2 — First message (after they accept)

Hi [Name], thanks for connecting!

Quick context: I run **Cyventra, Inc.** — a U.S. software corporation that automates one costly, repetitive process for small businesses at a fixed price ($1,500-$2,500), live in 1-2 weeks. No long contracts.

For [industry, e.g. law firms / clinics / contractors], the most common one we see is [specific pain, e.g. "new-client intake and follow-up" / "quote requests that sit unanswered for days"]. Is that something that eats up time at [Company]?

Happy to share how it'd work for you if it's relevant — no pressure either way.

https://cyventrasoft.com/start-sprint

---

## Step 3 — Follow-up (send if no reply after ~5-7 days)

Hi [Name] — following up in case this got buried. No worries if it's not a priority right now.

If it's ever useful, here's a quick look at how the sprint works: https://cyventrasoft.com/start-sprint

Either way, wishing you a good one!

---

**Rules of thumb:**
- One message at a time, sent by a human (you), not a bot.
- If they reply with any interest, move the conversation to a call — don't try to close over DM.
- If they don't reply after the follow-up, stop. Don't send a third message.
